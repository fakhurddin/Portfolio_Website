Overview

The problem

Passengers call around for departure times and only learn the fare, vehicle, seat and driver at the stop. Drivers run with empty seats, and owners manage vans and drivers by phone.

The solution

One app lists every trip with its time, fare per seat, vehicle and verified driver. Passengers pick an exact seat and track the ride live, in English or Urdu.

My role

Defined the problem, the three user groups and the rules, planned the workflows and database, and built the app with AI-assisted development.

Built for four roles

Passengers

Compare trips, pick an exact seat, know the driver before the ride and share live tracking with family.

Drivers

Get requests from people searching the route and run trips stop by stop. Cash fares, 0% commission for the first two months.

Vehicle owners

Add vans, drive them or assign a driver, and publish trips at their own fare within the route limit.

SabKi Sawari team

Verifies people and vehicles, sets fare limits per route and runs support.

Challenges solved

No double bookings

A seat someone else is booking shows as in progress and cannot be picked, so two passengers never hold the same seat.

Honest before launch

The app is not on Google Play yet, so nothing pretends trips are running: download buttons say coming soon and searches become route suggestions.

Demand before day one

Visitors suggest their route from 66 cities in Sindh. Only role and gender are asked, and every suggestion shows where to launch next.
