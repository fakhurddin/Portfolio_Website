Overview

The problem

Passengers call around for departure times and only learn the fare, vehicle, seat and driver at the stop. Drivers run with empty seats, and owners manage vans and drivers by phone.

The solution

One app lists every trip with its time, fare per seat, vehicle and verified driver. Passengers pick an exact seat and track the ride live, in English or Urdu.

My role

I defined the problem, the three user groups and the rules, planned the workflows and database, and built the app with AI-assisted development.

Who it’s for

Passengers

Before: Called around for departure times; fare, van, seat and driver were only known at the stop.

Now: Every trip in one list with time, fare per seat, vehicle and verified driver, and an exact seat.

Drivers

Before: Empty seats on many runs, and no way to see demand before reaching the stop.

Now: Seat requests from people searching the route, and every trip run stop by stop in the app.

Vehicle owners

Before: Vans, fares and drivers handled by phone, with no view of a trip once it left.

Now: Vans, drivers and trips in one place, with every booking and trip followed live.

What it does

Search and compare

Trips for a route and date side by side: departure, duration, vehicle, seats left and fare per seat.

Exact seat choice

A live seat map shows which seats are free, being booked or taken, with the price under each.

Know your driver

Driver name, rating and the exact vehicle are shown before the trip.

Live tracking and safety

The van on a map with every stop, an SOS button and a link that family can follow.

Driver app

Go online, accept or decline requests and run the trip stop by stop. Fares are paid in cash, with 0% commission for the first two months.

Owner tools

Add vans (each needs at least 3 of 10 listed facilities), drive or assign a driver, and publish trips within the route fare limit.

Screens

Key decisions

No double bookings

A seat someone else is booking shows as in progress and cannot be picked, so two passengers never hold the same seat.

Honest before launch

The app is not on Google Play yet, so the website does not pretend trips are running. Download buttons say coming soon, and searches are saved as route suggestions.

Checks inside the app

Drivers and owners only register interest on the website. CNIC, licence and vehicle papers are checked inside the app.

Where it is now

Today

The app is ready to launch on the Karachi to Hyderabad route. The pre-launch website is live in English and Urdu and collects route suggestions from 66 cities in Sindh.

What's next

Launch on Google Play, then in-app payments with JazzCash and Easypaisa, a seat preference for women, and new routes chosen from suggestion data.

How success is measured

Seats travelled each week, how many searches end in a booking, how full each van is, and how few trips are cancelled.
